# Elevate Lab Internship — Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

## 📌 Project Overview
The objective of this task is to transform raw retail operational data into an interactive visual story that delivers clear, actionable business insights. By focusing on dashboard design best practices—such as avoiding visual clutter, maintaining color consistency, and focusing on high-level KPIs—this dashboard provides an executive-level summary of performance metrics.

## 📊 Dataset Detail
* **File Name:** `SampleSuperstore.csv`
* **Data Scope:** 9,994 retail transaction records containing attributes across geographic regions, product segments, categories, sales volume, and overall profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
* **Business Intelligence Platform:** Power BI Desktop
* **Analytical Features:** Dynamic KPI cards, time-series analysis, and demographic categorical breakdown.

## 📈 Key Dashboard Insights
1. **Executive Performance:** High-level overview metrics immediately tracking total sales volume against overall earnings margins to establish a company performance baseline.
2. **Product Profitability:** A categorical distribution matrix highlighting **Technology** as a key driver of enterprise revenue compared to other categories.
3. **Geographical Demand:** Regional sales charts pinpointing the **West region** as the highest-performing operational territory.
4. **Market Segmentation:** Visual segmentation detailing profitability breakdown across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office operational groups.
